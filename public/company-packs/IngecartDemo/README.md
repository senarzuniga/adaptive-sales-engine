# Ingecart Demo Company Pack

This pack is a simulation clone of **Ingecart** saved as **Ingecart Demo**.

It preserves the original Ingecart baseline and adds a 7-hour operational simulation with recurring cycles:

- Inbound offer request every 15 minutes
- Customer call every 5 minutes (with follow-up email loop)
- Prospecting action every 7 minutes
- Real offer with full cost breakdown every 50 minutes
- Social/content post every hour
- Newsletter every 1.5 hours
- Market report every 1.5 hours
- Batch of 5 closed-loop actions every 0.8 hours
- Won-offer-to-project kickoff loop every 2 hours

## Usage

1. Open **Saved Companies**.
2. Click **Load Ingecart Demo Pack**.
3. Open **Ingecart Demo** and review Monitoring, Offer & Pricing, Marketing Content, Business Intelligence, and After-Sales modules.

# CTA Chief Architect Framework

This document defines the role, responsibilities, and processes for the CTA Enterprise Chief Architect.

Core responsibilities
- Define and maintain the Enterprise Architecture Hub and registries.
- Approve ADRs and canonical layouts.
- Own the governance engine and periodic architecture audits.
- Maintain the enterprise roadmap and priority list.

Processes
- Change requests must be submitted as ADRs in `Architecture/EnterpriseHub/adr/`.
- Repository and organization onboarding must be recorded in `repository_registry.yaml` and `organization_registry.yaml`.
- Agent lifecycle requests (promote, deprecate, retire) are made through the `agent_registry.yaml` entries and tracked via issues.

Governance cadence
- Weekly architecture review for active changes.
- Monthly enterprise audit and maturity reporting.
